BrewPOS Professional Roadmap - Senior Planning
=============================================

1. Navigation (new structure)
   - Dashboard (overview cards, quick actions)
   - POS / Cashier (fast sale, cart, payment)
   - Inventory (products, stock, low-stock alerts)
   - Sales (history, receipts, refunds)
   - Reports (daily/weekly/monthly, top products, cashier performance)
   - Customers (profiles, loyalty)
   - Settings (store info, users, roles, backup)

2. Sold tracking
   - Record every sale with items, quantities, prices, timestamps
   - Per-product sold count aggregation
   - Daily/weekly/monthly sold totals

3. Coffee ranking (most sold)
   - Leaderboard of top-selling coffee drinks
   - Filter by date range
   - Export ranking to CSV/PDF

4. Additional professional features
   - Role-based access (admin, manager, cashier)
   - Audit logs for all actions
   - Backup/restore database
   - Multi-branch support
   - Offline mode with sync
   - Receipt printing integration
   - Barcode scanning
   - Customer loyalty points
   - Tax and discount engine
   - Dark mode and mobile-responsive UI
   - Automated tests and CI/CD
   - Firestore security rules
